LEADERSHIP & VISION

Peculiar Abokondu

Founder & Creative Director

Alongside Divine Amedu, We channel the unfiltered pulse of the streets into unyielding high-fashion artistry. Prime Deluxe doesn't cater to mainstream trends; we forge iconic pieces for those who stand apart.

Hailing from Abuja, Nigeria, Peculiar Abokondu and Divine Amedu are the visionary founders behind Prime Deluxe. With a background in both streetwear and high fashion, they bring a unique perspective to the brand's creative direction.
